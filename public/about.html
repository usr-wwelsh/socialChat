Own your social network.

This site runs on socialChat — free, open-source software that anyone can self-host for their community. There's no central company, no ads, and no algorithm deciding what you see.

Why self-hosted?

  • Your community, your rules — you control moderation, access, and data.
  • No data harvesting — nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Decentralized — no single point of failure or control.
  • Real features — posts, profiles, friend lists, live chat, direct messages with end-to-end encryption, and more.

Run your own instance

Setting up your own socialChat takes a few minutes. You can host it on your own hardware, a VPS, Railway, or Docker — whatever fits your community.

$ git clone https://github.com/usr-wwelsh/socialChat
$ cd socialChat
$ bun install && bun start

That's it. A database is created automatically on first run.
